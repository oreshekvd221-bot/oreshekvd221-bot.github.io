Everything in the phone is clickable. These buttons jump to a state.

Design decisions

  • Search pill moves out of the header into its own full-width row: logo + pill + avatar do not fit in 390px.
  • Top bar scrolls away, pill and chips stay stuck to the top.
  • Search is a full-screen sheet with three cards. Choosing a destination or date auto-advances to the next card.
  • Dates are a list, not a calendar, because departures are fixed. Rows that do not fit the group size are disabled.
  • Live counter in the bottom button: "Show 4 trips".
  • All tap targets are at least 44px, body text 14px+.
